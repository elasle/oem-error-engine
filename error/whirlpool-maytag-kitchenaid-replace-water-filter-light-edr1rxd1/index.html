⚠️ Failure Symptom & Factory Service Manual Root Cause

Symptom on machine: The red or orange 'Replace Filter' / 'Order Filter' status light stays on, water dispenser flow drops to a slow stream, or ice cubes come out hollow and small.

Verified technical root cause: The activated carbon block inside the EveryDrop Filter 1 cartridge has reached its 200-gallon / 6-month pressure drop threshold, restricting water pressure to both the dispenser and ice maker fill valve.

🔧 Step-by-Step DIY Inspection & Replacement Checklist

  1. Open the filter door in the upper-right ceiling or bottom base grille of the refrigerator and push the old cartridge inward to pop it loose.
  2. Remove the protective cap from the new EDR1RXD1 filter, align the arrow, and push firmly until the door clicks shut.
  3. Hold the 'Filter Reset' or 'Light' button on the dispenser panel for 3 seconds until it beeps and flush 2 gallons of water to clear trapped air.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Replace Filter Red Light / Slow Water Dispenser Flow)

Why is my Whirlpool ice maker making tiny cubes even when water still comes out of the door?
The ice maker inlet solenoid only opens for a fixed 7.5-second timer—if a clogged EDR1RXD1 filter reduces water pressure by 50%, the ice mold only fills halfway during those 7.5 seconds.
