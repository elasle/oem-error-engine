⚠️ Failure Symptom & Factory Service Manual Root Cause

Symptom on machine: The LG dryer shuts off mid-cycle displaying d80, d90, or d95 and clothes stay damp even after cleaning the lint screen.

Verified technical root cause: Heavy lint buildup in the rigid wall duct OR a drifting blower housing outlet thermistor (OEM 6323EL2001B) / high-limit thermostat (6931EL3003D) misreading exhaust heat.

🔧 Step-by-Step DIY Inspection & Replacement Checklist

  1. Disconnect the rear 4-inch exhaust hose and run a 10-minute timed cycle venting into the room—if d80 disappears, sweep the wall/roof vent duct with a rotary brush.
  2. If d80/d90 still triggers with the hose disconnected, unplug the dryer and test thermistor 6323EL2001B on the blower housing (~10,000 ohms at room temperature).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Error d80 / d90 / d95 (Flow Sense 80%–95% Blocked))

What does LG d80 vs d90 mean on the Flow Sense display?
d80 means 80% exhaust airflow restriction detected; d90 and d95 mean 90%–95% restriction and the control board shuts down the heater to prevent a lint fire.
