⚠️ Failure Symptom & Factory Service Manual Root Cause

Symptom on machine: The Frigidaire or Electrolux front-load washer stops mid-rinse with water trapped behind the locked glass door and flashes E21 or E23.

Verified technical root cause: Coin or hair tie jammed inside the lower coin trap filter, or a burned-out drain pump motor assembly (OEM 5304524452 / 137221600).

🔧 Step-by-Step DIY Inspection & Replacement Checklist

  1. Drain standing water into a shallow pan via the small lower-right emergency drain hose, unscrew the coin trap cap, and clear any trapped coins or socks.
  2. If the coin trap is clean and the pump hums or reads open-circuit on a multimeter (normal: ~15–20 ohms), swap drain pump 5304524452.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Error E21 / E23 (Water Not Pumping Out / Drain Pump Relay))

How do I unlock my Electrolux washer door when E21 traps water inside?
Never pry the door open while full of water; first drain the tub via the small rubber tube behind the bottom kickplate or siphon from the rear drain hose, then unplug for 2 minutes.
