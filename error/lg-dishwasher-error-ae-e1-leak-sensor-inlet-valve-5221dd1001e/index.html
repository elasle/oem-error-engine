⚠️ Failure Symptom & Factory Service Manual Root Cause

Symptom on machine: The LG dishwasher chimes continuously, runs the drain pump even with the door shut, and flashes AE or E1 on the top display.

Verified technical root cause: Water in the bottom drip pan has lifted the styrofoam safety float switch, most frequently caused by a seeping water inlet valve (5221DD1001E) or loose diverter motor seal (4681ED3001D).

🔧 Step-by-Step DIY Inspection & Replacement Checklist

  1. Unplug the dishwasher and dry out the bottom base tray with a towel (or tilt forward 30 degrees) to reset the float switch.
  2. Run a quick rinse with the lower kickplate removed and a flashlight aimed under the left corner: if water drips from the blue/white inlet valve body, replace part 5221DD1001E.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Error AE / E1 (Water Leak Detected))

Why does LG Error AE come back right after using regular liquid dish soap?
Even 3 drops of Dawn hand dish soap creates foam that overflows the door corners into the bottom pan—if no part is leaking, run 2 cups of white vinegar or 2 tablespoons of vegetable oil to kill the suds.
